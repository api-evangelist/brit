If there's one franchise that has perfected the delicious moment when the detective gathers everyone in one room and finally reveals who did it, it's Knives Out . Since 2019, writer-director Rian Johnson and Daniel Craig (AKA the most charming Southern detective to ever wear a cravat) have given us three of the most fun mysteries in recent memory. We went from a sprawling New England mansion in Knives Out , to a billionaire's private Greek island in Glass Onion , to an isolated small-town parish in last year's Wake Up Dead Man .
