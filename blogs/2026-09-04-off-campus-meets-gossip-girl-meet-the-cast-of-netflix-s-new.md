If you've already rewatched Off-Campus multiple times and are looking for a new show to fill the void, then you definitely can't miss Crew Girl . The new show is coming to Netflix on September 10 and takes us inside the rowing team at an elite prep school. The show follows Teagan Tao (Miku Martineau), who has to switch to Easton Prep after a family scandal.
