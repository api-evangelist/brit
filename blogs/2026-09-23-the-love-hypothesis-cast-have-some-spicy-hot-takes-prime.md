The Love Hypothesis might be all about science, but the cast got to enjoy some beautiful art while filming the new Prime Video movie. "We went to Cirque du Soleil in Montreal in like the original tent where it all started," Jaboukie Young-White exclusively tells Brit + Co in our interview. "It was like Day of the Dead themed." Off set adventures are always a good way to bond with your cast mates, and it's clear this cast bonded very well — their friendship radiates through our call!
