I’ve always been a fan of all-inclusive resorts . When I finally obtained my passport at the age of 26, I never looked back, hopping from country to country, from the crashing shores of Los Cabos to the stark Pitons of St. Lucia, soaking in bottomless mimosas and 24/7 room service, thinking, ‘How could life get any better?’ Until it didn’t.
