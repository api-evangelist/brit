Navigating any new diet can be a challenge, but luckily, figuring out what you can (and can’t) eat on a vegan meal plan isn’t that hard. You’ll have to say goodbye to meat, eggs , honey, and dairy, and you’ll definitely need to hone your label-reading skills. Other than that, though, there’s a world of vegan recipes just waiting for you!
