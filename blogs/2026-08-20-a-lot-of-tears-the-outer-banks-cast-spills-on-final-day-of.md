Outer Banks season 5 is on Netflix now, after an incredibly emotional season 4 ending, where JJ (Rudy Pankow) died at the hand of his biological father Chandler Groff. And the new episodes leave the Pogues to pick up the pieces. "Obviously for Pope and John B., they lost their best friend, their third amigo, and so I think they're really mourning the loss of him," Chase Stokes tells Brit + Co exclusively.
