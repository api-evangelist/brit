When celebrity design firm House of Rolison takes on a historic property, they don’t just renovate — they resurrect. Their latest project, a 1930s estate tucked in the Hollywood Hills, is a masterclass in how to honor a home’s past without sacrificing modern comfort. Principals Amanda Leigh and Taylor Hahn leaned in on the vintage bones of the 1938-era home that had been stripped of its soul by a developer.
