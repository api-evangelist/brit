It's no secret that Lorelai Gilmore loves coffee, so it makes total sense that when actress Lauren Graham partnered with Bob's Red Mill, the recipe they developed together has coffee at the center! The collab, ahead of National Good Neighbor Day on September 28, highlights the idea of what they're calling "Moregetherness" (more + togetherness). It sounds like the perfect idea ahead of the holidays and all the IRL connections, homemade food, and time spent with the people we love.
