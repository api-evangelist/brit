Jessica Chastain is finally returning to the small screen in the Netflix drama Heartland . After her Apple thriller The Savant was indefinitely postponed, I've been dying to see her onscreen again, and her new movie Heartland sounds like the exact kind of thing I've been missing since Yellowstone , 1923 , and Ransom Canyon season 2 ended. If you're missing Yellowstone , you've got to check out Jessica Chastain's Netflix thriller-mystery-drama Heartland on Netflix.
