There's a particular kind of beauty that comes with dawn in New York City. Whether the sunrise reflects off the river, millions of windowpanes, or iconic skyscrapers, it feels like you're bathed in light no matter where you go. The beautiful weather and sunny, clear sky is one thing people remember most about the early morning of September 11, 2001.
