B.K. Borison is releasing a brand new book this February that is the perfect cozy read to add to your bookshelf: Longtime Listener . Not only is the novel the third piece of her Heartstrings trilogy alongside First-Time Caller and And Now, Back to You , but it's also the third book inspired by Meg Ryan 's most iconic movies.
