Dakota Fanning and Orlando Bloom might not have starred in a movie together, but they have shared a very memorable moment on camera. In 2002, Dakota won Best Young Actor/Actress at the Critics' Choice Awards for her role in I Am Sam (opposite Michelle Pfeiffer and Sean Penn), but the 7-year-old was too short to reach the microphone. So, Orlando Bloom picked her up so she could give her speech — and the moment continues to go absolutely viral.
