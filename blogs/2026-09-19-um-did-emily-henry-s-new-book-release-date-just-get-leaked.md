It's been over a year since Emily Henry published Great Big Beautiful Life in April 2025, and she just gave us the update we've been waiting for: she's publishing a new book and it's coming in 2027! While on Jenna Bush Hager's podcast Open Book with Jenna podcast, Emily Henry teased her brand new book, and even though she couldn't say much, she did confirm a few things: it's a straightforward romance, it's coming next summer, and it's influenced by The Holiday .
