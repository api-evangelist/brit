Leighton Meester and Jared Padalecki are starring in a brand new holiday rom-com called Guarding Stars based on Katherine Center's beloved book The Bodyguard . This cast is almost too good to believe, and this is the perfect movie for anyone who can't get enough of old school TV shows like Gossip Girl and Gilmore Girls . And not only are these two iconic stars leading the cast, but they're joined by some of your recent favorite Netflix actors too!
