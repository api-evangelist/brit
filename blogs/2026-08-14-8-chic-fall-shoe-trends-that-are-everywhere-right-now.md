It's the season of change, which means switching up your fall footwear is officially in order. Fall, after all, is when your favorite styles are on full display — when they can still be paired with shorter hemlines, aren't yet shrouded by winter layers, and don't quite need to be fully equipped to deal with harsh weather just yet. From slingbacks to scrunchy boots, it's time to have some fun with your wardrobe by way of these eight irresistible fall shoe trends.
