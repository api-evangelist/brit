Sterling Point is so compelling, and has so many intriguing characters, that it feels exactly like the kind of TV adaptation BookTok would go crazy over. But perhaps the best detail about this new summer romance show is that it's not actually based on a book! The series follows Annie (Ella Rubin) as she travels to the island of Sterling Point in Canada after learning she inherited it from her estranged, adoptive grandfather.
