Crew Girl is returning to Netflix with season 2, the streamer announced on September 22. The show debuted on September 10, and follows Teagan Tao (Miku Martineau), who has to switch schools after a major family scandal. Now she lives on the East coast, and attends a prestigious prep school — with no girls' rowing team.
