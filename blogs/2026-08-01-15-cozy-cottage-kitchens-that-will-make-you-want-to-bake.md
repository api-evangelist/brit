All white kitchens have had their moment, but designers are now leaning into warmer, earthier tones that feel much more inviting. Enter the cottage kitchen — the coziest antidote to the cold, ultra-modern spaces of recent years. With vintage charm, natural textures and a cozy mix of old and new, this style brings comfort and character back to the heart of the home.
