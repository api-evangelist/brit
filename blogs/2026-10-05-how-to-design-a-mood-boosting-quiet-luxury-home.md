In a design moment dominated by bold color, quiet luxury proves that thoughtfully placed earthy hues and natural light can still set the mood of a home and create a calm, restful space. Designed by ALLPRACE founders Shanty Wijaya and James Mulyadi, an LA-based house is a study in quiet luxury, a style focused on craftsmanship, restraint, and materials that improve with age. Here, the design duo pair the aesthetic with something even more elemental: a deliberate connection to nature.
