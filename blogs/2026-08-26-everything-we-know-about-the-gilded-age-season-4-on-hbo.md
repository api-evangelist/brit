The Gilded Age season 4 is officially premiering this November, so it won't be long before we're able to reenter the world of high society New York. The new episodes of Julian Fellows' HBO period drama will open after the cliffhanger ending in season 3 : George (Morgan Spector) and Bertha's (Carrie Coon) marriage is on the rocks, while Larry (Harry Richardson) and Marian (Louisa Jacobson) are hesitantly rebuilding their relationship. Meanwhile, Peggy (Denée Benton) and William Kirkland (Jordan Donica) are getting married!
