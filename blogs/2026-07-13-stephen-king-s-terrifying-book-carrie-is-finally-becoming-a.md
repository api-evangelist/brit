There's a very good chance you're familiar with Stephen King 's Carrie (even if it was just the Carrie: The Musical episode of Riverdale ). Well, for the first time ever, the beloved novel is getting adapted for TV thanks to Prime Video — and we finally have the first look. The story is one of King's most well known, and it's safe to assume this adaptation will be just as scary, and memorable as the others.
