The first trailer for the brand new Harry Potter TV show is here — and it looks just as magical as HBO promised. Despite the fact it's only been 15 years since the decade-long global phenomenon (starring Daniel Radcliffe, Emma Watson , and Rupert Grint) ended, we're already strapping in for another take on the series. I do think we could have waited another 10 years before taking another crack at the story, but the kids playing Harry , Ron, and Hermione are so cute that as soon as the trailer started, I knew I'd be watching.
