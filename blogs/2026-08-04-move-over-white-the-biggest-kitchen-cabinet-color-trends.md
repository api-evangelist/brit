If you’re itching to fall in love with your kitchen again, a color swap is the easiest fix. Four years after coating my own cabinets in Benjamin Moore’s Adriatic Sea , I'm ready to lean into a more earthy vibe — and it turns out designers agree. "The biggest trend we’re seeing for kitchens and kitchen cabinets is homeowners looking to add more color and turn away from all-white kitchens and gray kitchens/kitchen cabinets," says Sue Wadden, Sherwin-Williams Director of Color Marketing.
